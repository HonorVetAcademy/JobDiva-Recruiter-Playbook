Frequently Asked Questions

The ten questions every new recruiter asks in the first week — with a pointer to the full chapter for each.

What’s the difference between Search Candidates and Talent Search?
They’re the same 8-tab search screen — JobDiva just lists it under two menu items. This book calls the chapter “Talent Search,” but the screens themselves say “Search Candidates.” See Chapter 5.
Do I really have to pick a Resume Source every time?
Yes — on every new candidate, no exceptions. It tells the whole team where this person came from. See Chapter 11.
What happens if I accidentally create a duplicate candidate?
Their history splits across two profiles and the team loses track of notes, licenses, and past submissions. Always search by email, then phone, then name before creating anyone new. See Chapter 2.
How long do I “own” a candidate?
7 days after you take ownership, or 30 days after you submit them to a job. If they go quiet before then, ownership opens back up to the team. See Chapter 11.
Two recruiters want to submit the same candidate — who wins?
Whoever has an active RTR (Right to Represent) on file for that client has first claim. Always check Activities/Pipeline before submitting. See Chapters 8 & 9.
What does “Harvest: Not Scheduled” mean on a job?
The Harvester isn’t running for that job yet — usually because Qualifications isn’t filled in. Fix it from the job’s Qualifications tab. See Chapter 10.
Where do I check if a candidate’s license is about to expire?
Their profile’s left panel Licenses table, or filter for it directly with Expiring After on the Licenses tab. See Chapters 5 & 6.
What’s the difference between My Jobs and My Jobs as Primary?
My Jobs is every job you touch, sourcing or recruiting. My Jobs as Primary is just the ones where you’re the lead recruiter. See Chapter 3.
Can I undo a submission?
Not from your side — talk to your manager right away. This is exactly why Step 8 (checking Activities/Pipeline first) matters so much. See Chapter 9.
Who do I tell if a candidate contacted another recruiter outside JobDiva?
Bring it to the Admin with proof of the conversation — that recruiter can claim TWC. See Chapter 11.
